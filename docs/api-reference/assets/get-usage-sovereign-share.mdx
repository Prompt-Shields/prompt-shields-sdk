---
title: Get Sovereign Share
openapi: get /partner/v1/usage/sovereign-share
---

The share of calls and of spend served in a sovereign jurisdiction: `EEA` or an
EEA country code, as listed in `sovereign_jurisdictions`. Shares are given in
total and per sensitivity level, so you can check that confidential and
restricted traffic stays in the EEA.

<Note>
Calls that did not report a `served_jurisdiction` are left out of both sides of
each share. The jurisdiction is the one your route policy states for the tier
that served the call, so a US-headquartered provider's EU region counts as `EEA`
when the policy says so. US CLOUD Act exposure is not stored per call: this is a
share by jurisdiction, not a sovereignty guarantee or a legal assessment. To keep
exposed providers out of sensitive traffic, set `us_cloud_act_exposure: false` in
the policy's `data_policy` rules.
</Note>
