---
title: Get Sovereign Share
openapi: get /partner/v1/usage/sovereign-share
---

The share of calls and of spend served in a sovereign jurisdiction: `EEA` or an
EEA country code, as listed in `sovereign_jurisdictions`. Shares are given in
total and per sensitivity level, so you can check that confidential and
restricted traffic stays in the EEA.

<Note>
Calls that did not report a `served_jurisdiction` are left out of both sides of
each share. US CLOUD Act exposure is not stored per call, so this is a share by
jurisdiction, not a legal assessment. The model catalog lists each provider's
`us_cloud_act_exposure`.
</Note>
